Program & Financial Data Integration Platform

Team lead · Enterprise program

Program, financial, and operational data lived in disconnected systems, leaving leadership without a single reliable source of truth and hiding broken business processes behind manual reporting. Led the design and rollout of a governed data integration and BI platform: automated pipelines from project-tracking, timekeeping, and cost systems into a business intelligence layer, certified datasets with data governance and access controls, persona-based dashboards for finance and budget leadership, dev/test/prod environments, and a support ticketing system and user guide to drive adoption. Surfaced revenue that manual reporting had been leaving on the table, and removed a recurring monthly data-entry burden from a large population of managers.

Scaling Atlassian Delivery & Sustainment Reporting

Cross-functional lead & Scrum Master · Multi-team delivery

Leadership had no consistent way to see delivery timelines, service desk volume, or incident patterns across two separate Atlassian environments serving different audiences. Coached three cross-functional teams operating under two frameworks at once — Agile Scrum for new project and feature work, an ITIL-based demand pipeline for sustainment — and built an OKR roadmap framework plus standardized incident capture so leadership could finally see delivery volume, resolution times, and root-cause patterns. Compressed delivery cycles from many months to a matter of weeks, brought ad hoc request turnaround down to a same-week standard, and sustained consistently high customer satisfaction across a multi-year delivery portfolio.

Atlassian Tooling Modernization & Service Delivery Transformation

Cross-functional lead · Enterprise Atlassian platform

An enterprise Atlassian ecosystem serving thousands of users suffered from long cycle times and inconsistent adoption, with development tickets and project deliverables both running well beyond target. Led standardization across Jira Software, Jira Service Management, Rich Filters, Assets, Confluence, and Bitbucket, applied ITIL practices, and introduced governance, standardized workflows, unified roadmaps, and consistent outage reporting, partnering with stakeholders to define shared KPIs. Cut project delivery time and ad-hoc request resolution substantially, and raised platform availability against an already-high baseline.

Agile Transformation Coaching Engagement

Agile Coach · Contract engagement

A large engineering organization had adopted Scrum in name without understanding the value behind it, and was confused about how their process differed from ITIL — in practice they had drifted into ad hoc, ITIL-like habits without realizing it. Diagnosed that pure Scrum didn't fit their mix of planned project work and ad hoc support demand, introduced a Scrumban approach, shifted focus from story-point estimation toward prioritizing by business impact, and coached the team on the actual value behind each ceremony. Moved the organization from misapplied Scrum to a right-fit Scrumban model inside a short coaching contract.

Digital Food Assistance Request & Fulfillment System

Solo contributor · Volunteer leadership role · Apr 2026

As a volunteer congregation leader overseeing temporary financial and food relief requests, intake was entirely manual — a paper form filled out by members, handed off or texted, relayed to leadership for approval, then keyed into the organization's food order system, averaging 4 days per request. Built a web app that digitizes the intake form, captures responses into Google Sheets, and automatically notifies the approving leader and a backup team the moment a request is submitted, so orders can be entered the same day. Cut average request-to-fulfillment time from 4 days to a few hours; now being prepared to scale to 4 additional congregations.
