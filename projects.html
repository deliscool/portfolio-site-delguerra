Program & Financial Data Integration Platform

Team lead · Team of 5 · Mar–Jun 2026

Program, financial, and operational data lived in disconnected systems, leaving leadership without a single reliable source of truth and hiding broken business processes behind manual reporting. Led the design and rollout of a governed data integration and BI platform: automated pipelines from project-tracking, timekeeping, and cost systems into Superset, certified datasets with data governance and access controls, persona-based dashboards for finance and budget directors, dev/test/prod environments, and a support ticketing system and user guide to drive pilot adoption. Recovered $100K in unbilled revenue and eliminated 100+ hours/month of manager data entry across 30+ managers.

Scaling Atlassian Delivery & Sustainment Reporting

Cross-functional lead & Scrum Master · 3 teams, 15 people · Jun 2023–Dec 2025

Leadership had no consistent way to see delivery timelines, service desk volume, or incident patterns across two separate Atlassian environments serving different program audiences. Coached three cross-functional teams operating under two frameworks at once — Agile Scrum for new project and feature work, an ITIL-based demand pipeline for sustainment — and built an OKR roadmap framework plus standardized incident capture so leadership could see project counts, resolution times, and root-cause patterns. Project delivery dropped from ~8 months to 1–2 months; ad hoc ticket resolution went from 3 weeks to a 2.5-business-day average; CSAT reached 4.5/5 across 50 projects delivered in two years.

Atlassian Tooling Modernization & Service Delivery Transformation

Cross-functional lead · Jan–Nov 2025

An internal Atlassian ecosystem supporting roughly 1,850 Jira, 1,400 Confluence, and 100 Bitbucket users suffered from long cycle times and inconsistent adoption — development tickets averaged 46.7 business days and project deliverables 77.1. Led standardization across Jira Software, Jira Service Management, Rich Filters, Assets, Confluence, and Bitbucket, applied ITIL practices, and introduced governance, standardized workflows, unified roadmaps, and consistent outage reporting, partnering with stakeholders to define shared KPIs. Project delivery improved from 77.1 to 45.72 business days (~40% faster), ad-hoc request resolution fell from 20–40 days to 4.79 days, and app uptime rose from 98.94% to 99.31%.

Agile Transformation Coaching Engagement

Agile Coach · AgileDad contract · ~50-person engineering org

A large nonprofit's engineering team had adopted Scrum in name without understanding the value behind it, and was confused about how their process differed from ITIL — in practice they had drifted into ad hoc, ITIL-like habits without realizing it. Diagnosed that pure Scrum didn't fit their mix of planned project work and ad hoc support demand, introduced a Scrumban approach, shifted focus from story-point estimation toward prioritizing by business impact, and coached the team on the actual value behind each ceremony. Transitioned a ~50-person engineering organization from misapplied Scrum to a right-fit Scrumban model within a 3-month contract.

Digital Food Assistance Request & Fulfillment System

Solo contributor · Volunteer leadership role · Apr 2026

As a volunteer congregation leader overseeing temporary financial and food relief requests, intake was entirely manual — a paper form filled out by members, handed off or texted, relayed to leadership for approval, then keyed into the organization's food order system, averaging 4 days per request. Built a web app that digitizes the intake form, captures responses into Google Sheets, and automatically notifies the approving leader and a backup team the moment a request is submitted, so orders can be entered the same day. Cut average request-to-fulfillment time from 4 days to a few hours; now being prepared to scale to 4 additional congregations.
